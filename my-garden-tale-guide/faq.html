🌱 Getting Started

Can I really play My Garden Tale for free? ▼
Yes! The game is completely free to download and play. You can progress steadily without spending real money. The key is to play smart: prioritize land expansion and tool upgrades, and always check orders before planting. Gems can be earned through daily logins, events, and codes — you don't need to buy them.
Is the game really free or are there paywalls? ▼
No hard paywalls. Every feature (planting, orders, guilds, pets) is accessible for free. The game makes money through optional in-app purchases (gems, skins, boosters). You can play indefinitely without spending — it just takes longer at higher levels. Think of purchases as "time savers" not "necessities."
What devices can I play on? ▼
My Garden Tale is available on:
  • iPhone / iPad — App Store
  • Android phones & tablets — Google Play
  • PC — via Android emulators (BlueStacks, LDPlayer)
Your progress can sync across devices if you link your account (Facebook, Google Play, or Apple ID).

💰 Gold & Gem Strategy

What's the best way to earn gold fast? ▼
Order fulfillment is #1. Selling flowers through orders pays 3-5x more than the shop. Beyond that:
  • Plant fast-growing flowers (daisy 40min, sunflower 1h) to generate constant income
  • Keep expanding your land — more plots = more flowers = more orders
  • Complete daily guild quests (2,000+ gold per week)
  • Use pets with gold bonus (Fox "Ruby", Hamster "Peanut")
Where should I spend gems? ▼
Gem priority order:
  1. Limited-time events — best value, often 2x-3x gem bonuses
  2. Land expansion — if no events are running
  3. Building speed-ups — only when close to max level
  4. Season pass — includes rare outfits + bonus gems
  5. Avoid: Regular gacha and shop purchases
Never spend gems on regular gacha unless it's a guaranteed reward event. You'll waste 90% of your gems on items you don't need.
How do I save gems without spending them? ▼
  • Daily login — check daily for gem rewards
  • Order rewards — some orders give gem payouts
  • Redeem codes — check our codes page daily
  • Events & challenges — weekly events give generous gem rewards
  • Guild quests — complete all 3 daily guild quests
  • Pet exploration — send pets out regularly

🌸 Flowers & Planting

How do flowers bloom? What do they need? ▼
Flowers need two things:
  • Time — each flower has a fixed growth time (30min to 12h)
  • Water — tap the watering can to water each plot. Some flowers need water every 30-60 minutes
Upgrading your watering can (LV3 = water 3 grids at once, LV5 = auto-water) saves hours of tapping per day.
How do I unlock new flower types? ▼
Unlock flowers by:
  • Reaching the required level — each flower has a minimum level (e.g., Rose = Lv.1, Tulip = Lv.5, Cherry Blossom = Lv.10)
  • Unlocking the Seed Library — the main unlock point for new flower types
  • Event rewards — some rare flowers are event-only unlocks
The Seed Library is your #3 gold spending priority — new flowers = more orders = more income.
Why do my flowers keep dying? ▼
Flowers die from not being watered. Each flower type has a watering interval (shown in-game). If you don't water within that time, the flower wilts and must be replanted. Solutions:
  • Upgrade your watering can — higher levels auto-water and expand range
  • Set phone reminders — especially for long-growth flowers
  • Get the Bird "Tweety" pet — alerts when flowers are ready
  • Don't plant more than you can water — manage your capacity

📋 Orders & Events

How do daily orders work? ▼
Every day you get 5-8 random orders from the guild board. Each order needs specific flowers:
  • Check orders first before planting anything
  • Plant the flowers needed and wait for them to grow
  • Harvest and deliver before the deadline for bonus gold/gems
  • Orders refresh daily — new orders appear each morning
  • S- and A-priority orders give gem rewards — prioritize these
Check our Orders Guide for full strategy.
What happens if I miss an order deadline? ▼
Missed orders simply expire and give no reward. You don't lose anything (no penalties), but you miss out on the gold/gem payout. Tips to avoid missing:
  • Check orders first thing in the morning
  • Plant long-growth flowers (orchids, peonies) early in the day
  • Use speed-up items on critical orders
  • Keep a buffer of fast-growing flowers (daisy, sunflower) for last-minute orders
How do special events work? ▼
Events are limited-time activities that offer special rewards (rare outfits, bonus gems, exclusive flowers):
  • Length: Usually 3-7 days
  • How to join: Tap the event banner on the main screen
  • Tasks: Complete specific goals (e.g., "Plant 50 sunflowers")
  • Rewards: Progress-based — complete more tasks = better rewards
  • Preparation: Stock up on seeds and speed-ups before the event starts
Always prepare before events: save gems for event purchases and keep your land expanded. Events give the best value for your effort!

🎁 Codes & Rewards

How do I redeem gift codes? ▼
  1. Open My Garden Tale
  2. Tap the 📬 Mail icon
  3. Tap "Redeem Code" at the bottom
  4. Enter the code exactly (case-sensitive) and confirm
  5. Collect rewards from your in-game mailbox
Check our codes page for the latest working codes!
Why is my code expired or not working? ▼
Gift codes have expiration dates set by the developers. Once expired, they can't be redeemed. Reasons include:
  • Time expired — codes are typically valid for 1-4 weeks
  • Usage limit — each code can only be used once per account
  • Regional restrictions — some codes only work in certain regions
We update our codes page daily and mark expired codes clearly. Bookmark our codes page and check regularly!

🐾 Pets & Guild

How do I get pets? ▼
Pets are obtained through:
  • Pet Shop — spend gems to unlock new pets
  • Event rewards — rare and legendary pets often appear in events
  • Pet exploration — sending pets out can unlock new species
  • Season pass — some pets are season pass rewards
Start with common pets (Cat, Dog) for basic bonuses, then save gems for legendary pets (Dragon, Phoenix, Unicorn) later.
Which pet is best for income? ▼
Gold-focused pets:
  • 🦊 Fox "Ruby" — +15% gold bonus (best income pet)
  • 🐹 Hamster "Peanut" — +5% gold per flower (stacks with Fox)
  • 🐾 Dragon "Ember" — 20% gold bonus (legendary, game-changer)
Overall best: Combine Fox + Hamster + Dragon for maximum income. These three stack for a total of ~40%+ gold bonus.
How do I join a guild? ▼
  1. Reach Lv. 5 (takes about 1 day of casual play)
  2. Tap the ⭐ Guild icon on the main screen
  3. Choose "Join Guild" and browse available guilds
  4. Use the level filter to find a guild matching your level
  5. Apply and wait for approval (most guilds approve instantly)
Check our Guild Guide for tips on choosing the right guild!
Pro tip: Join a guild on Day 1. The passive bonuses compound over time and become essential for late-game progress.

⚙️ General Questions

Can I reset my progress and start over? ▼
It depends on the game version. Some versions allow a fresh start through settings, but it usually requires reaching a certain level first. Be aware: resetting means losing everything — all your progress, pets, outfits, and guild rank. We recommend not resetting unless absolutely necessary, as the game is designed to be enjoyable at every level.
Can I trade flowers or items with other players? ▼
Not directly. My Garden Tale doesn't have a player-to-player trading system. However, you can:
  • Share codes with friends to help them get rewards
  • Join the same guild and coordinate strategies
  • Participate in guild events together for better rewards
  • Some versions may have a guild donation system for contributing items
