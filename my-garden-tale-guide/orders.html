🎯 Why Orders Are Everything

Orders are the most important system in My Garden Tale. They determine what you should plant each day, and fulfilling them gives you 3-5x more gold than selling to the shop.

Golden Rule: Check orders BEFORE you plant. 90% of progress comes from planting the right flowers at the right time.

⚡ Order Fulfillment Strategy

🌱 Step 1: Check All Orders First

Don't rush to plant. Look at all 5-8 orders and identify the S/A priority ones. Note which flowers appear multiple times.

🌻 Step 2: Plant High-Demand Flowers First

If 3 orders need roses, plant all roses together. Same for sunflowers and tulips — they grow fast and appear most often.

⏰ Step 3: Check Deadlines

Prioritize orders with the shortest deadline first. Fast-growing flowers fill quickly; slow ones need advance planting.

💰 Step 4: Balance Fast + High-Value

Mix fast-growing flowers (daisy, sunflower) for steady income with high-value ones (rose, orchid) for big payouts.

🔄 Step 5: Harvest & Sell Before New Orders Refresh

Orders refresh daily. Finish as many as possible before the refresh to maximize your daily gold.

💎 Step 6: Save Gem Rewards for Events

Don't spend gem order rewards immediately. Save them for limited-time events with better exchange rates.

🚫 Order Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Planting without checking orders — wastes time and gold on flowers nobody needs today
  2. Selling to shop instead of fulfilling orders — orders pay 3-5x more than the open market
  3. Ignoring deadlines — expired orders mean lost gold and gems
  4. Planting only one type — diversify to cover all order combinations
  5. Not saving speed-ups for order completion — use speed-ups to rush high-value orders
  6. Forgetting about long-growth flowers — orchids and peonies take hours; plant them early
The #1 mistake: selling flowers to the shop instead of fulfilling orders. Every flower sold to the shop is 80% less gold than fulfilling the order.
