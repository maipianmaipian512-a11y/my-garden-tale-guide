💡 Flower Strategy Tips

  • Always plant for orders — the 5 S/A tier flowers (Rose, Sunflower, Orchid, Lotus, Lavender) cover 80% of daily orders
  • Keep a buffer of Daisy & Sunflower — they grow fast (under 1h) and fill any gap in orders
  • Long-growth flowers = plant early — Orchid (4h), Pine (8h), and Bonsai (12h) must be planted well ahead of order deadlines
  • ROI matters more than total payout — a 1h flower at 1,800g (Sunflower) beats an 8h flower at 6,000g (Pine) in pure efficiency
  • Event flowers are worth the wait — Ghost Orchid pays 8,000g but only during events
  • Don't ignore seed costs — a flower that costs 600g seed for 1,500g payout (Stone Flower) has terrible ROI
Pro tip: Focus your garden on growing the top 6 S/A tier flowers efficiently. Have 50+ plots dedicated to Rose, Sunflower, Tulip, Orchid, Lavender, and Lily. Fill remaining space with Daisy/Wild Wheat for quick income. This covers 90% of all orders you'll encounter.
