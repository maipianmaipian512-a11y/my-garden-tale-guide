🏡 What Is the Pet Courtyard?

The Pet Courtyard is a separate area where you collect and care for pets. Each pet has unique abilities that help you in the garden:

  • 🌸 Auto-Harvest — some pets auto-collect ripe flowers
  • ⚡ Speed Boost — reduce flower growth time
  • 💰 Gold Bonus — increase gold earned from orders
  • 🌈 Special — rare pets have unique abilities like rare flower discovery
Pets are passive helpers. Once assigned, they work automatically — no daily maintenance needed beyond feeding and upgrading.

🏠 Furniture Upgrades

Furniture in the Pet Courtyard boosts your pets' abilities:

🏠 Pet House

Level up to unlock more pet slots. Lv.1 = 1 slot, Lv.5 = 5 slots. Essential to max.

🥣 Food Bowl

Increases pet food efficiency. Each level gives +10% exploration rewards.

🛏️ Pet Bed

Reduces pet cooldown time. Faster exploration = more rewards per day.

🎾 Toy Chest

Boosts pet happiness. Happy pets earn 15% more gold and gems.

🌳 Garden Tree

Decorative but functional. Adds passive gold generation to the courtyard.

💧 Water Fountain

Auto-waters nearby flowers. Reduces manual watering by 20%.

Upgrade the Pet House first (more slots = more pets = more bonuses). Then upgrade Food Bowl for exploration boost.

💡 Pet Strategy Tips

  1. Start with common pets — they give basic bonuses that help early progress
  2. Save gems for legendary pets — the Dragon and Phoenix pets are game-changers
  3. Always explore — even 2-hour trips are better than nothing
  4. Match pets to your playstyle — gold-focused? Get Fox + Hamster. Growth-focused? Get Dog + Phoenix
  5. Upgrade furniture before getting new pets — unlocked slots let you stack bonuses
  6. Don't ignore rare pets — even uncommon pets like Rabbit (rare flower finder) are worth it
Legendary pets (Phoenix, Unicorn, Dragon) cost a lot of gems to unlock. Don't rush them — focus on gold income first, then invest in legends.
