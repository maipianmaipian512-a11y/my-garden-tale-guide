📌 Today's Priority Tips

🌸 Order Priority Rule: Always Check Before Planting

My Garden Tale's core loop: check daily orders → plant flowers the orders need → harvest → sell bouquets. Many beginners plant first and sell later, which wastes time and money.

  • Always check your order board before planting new seeds
  • Prioritize flowers that appear in multiple orders today
  • Skip planting rare flowers with zero orders — they take too long for low payout
  • Save your gems for events, not regular shop purchases
Pro tip: Flowers used in the most orders usually give the best return on investment. Check the Guild Page page for a full priority chart.
