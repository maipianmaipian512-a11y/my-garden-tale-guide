🏰 What Is a Guild?

Guilds are the social core of My Garden Tale. When you join a guild, you get:

  • Daily Guild Quests — contribute flowers to earn bonus gold, gems, and boosters
  • Guild Events — weekly competitive events with exclusive rewards
  • Guild Bonuses — passive boosts to gold gain, flower growth speed, and gem drops
  • Guild Chat — coordinate with other players for tips and trades
Join a guild on Day 1. The bonuses compound over time and become essential for late-game progress.

🎯 How to Choose a Guild

Not all guilds are equal. Here's what to look for:

✅ What to Look For

  • Active members — check if guild members log in daily (look at the member list)
  • High activity rate — guild with 20+ daily contributors
  • Helpful atmosphere — members who share tips and codes
  • Compatible level range — join a guild where your level matches the average
  • Guild level — higher level = better passive bonuses

❌ What to Avoid

  • Dead guilds — less than 5 daily contributors
  • Toxic communities — members who spam or mock new players
  • Overly competitive — if you play casually, don't join an extreme PvP guild
  • Language barriers — make sure you can communicate

💡 Guild Tips

  • Donate daily — even small contributions add up to big bonuses over time
  • Participate in every event — event rewards are 5-10x normal rewards
  • Chat with your guild — they'll share codes and strategies you'd miss otherwise
  • Don't leave a good guild — switching costs you time and you'll lose your contribution rank
  • Be a good member — active, helpful members get more help in return
  • Contribute rare flowers — they give bonus guild points and boost your rank
Leaving a guild has a cooldown. Make sure you want to join before committing — finding a good guild takes time.
